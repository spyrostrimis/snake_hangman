# Snakes and Hangmans

Versions of one game, built with [GameMaker](https://gamemaker.io) while learning it: steer a snake into the letters of a hidden word. A right letter reveals every copy of it and makes the snake grow. A wrong letter costs one of six chances. Six misses, a wall or your own tail end the run.

Each folder is a complete GameMaker project. Open its `.yyp` file in GameMaker, or build it from the command line with [GM-CLI](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@gamemaker/gm-cli) (`npx @gamemaker/gm-cli run` inside the folder).

| Folder | Look |
| --- | --- |
| [`snake_hangman/`](snake_hangman/) | v1: neon pixel art, with a pixel font drawn in code. |
| [`snake_hangman2/`](snake_hangman2/) | v2: dressed as the Hall of Fame page of [Hangman: Rescue Mission](https://hangman.spyrostrimis.com), with its moon background, Bruno Ace SC, colours, Artsy and an arcade-style local Hall of Fame. |
| [`snake_hangman4/`](snake_hangman4/) | v4, "Bubble Rounds": a tribute to the bubble arcades of the 80s, and above all to Taito's *Bubble Bobble* (1986). It runs at 320 × 240 shown at 3×, with brick rounds in a new colour each time, letter bubbles, a word column and six chances, lives, treats, a spiky enemy called Fizz, "HURRY UP!", a gap in the walls to wrap through, the scroll between rounds, a chiptune made in code, scanlines and a Best 5 table with initials. All the art, characters, rounds and music are original. |
| [`snake_hangman5/`](snake_hangman5/) | v5, "Jungle Jam": a side-scrolling platformer and a tribute to the 90s PC platformers, *Jazz Jackrabbit 2* above all. The snake runs, jumps, glides on a spinning tail, knocks out creatures and pulls in items with its tongue, and curls into a ball to smash cracked blocks. Each level hides the letters of a secret word; wrong letters are misses, and the exit door only opens when the word is complete. A boss guards each exit and holds one letter of the word: Big Cheese, a boxer mouse who charges and lobs cheese, and the Hothead Hen, who hops, pecks and rains feathers. Rare eggs restore two hearts, and you can watch the snake swallow them. It is built with real GameMaker structures: a runtime tilemap and tileset, parallax background layers, an asset layer of decorations, a camera, parent and child objects, a bitmap font made from a sprite, particle systems and streamed Ogg music. All characters, levels and art are original. |
| [`snake_hangman7/`](snake_hangman7/) | v7, "Embersand": a break from snakes. A real-time strategy game and a tribute to the desert strategy games of the early 90s, *Dune II* above all, in its own world: harvest glowing lumen crystals from the dunes, build a base on the rock (power, refinery, barracks, motor works, radar, turrets, walls), train infantry and tanks from three build queues, and beat a computer opponent that rebuilds its base and attacks in waves. Three missions with briefings from K-7, a survey drone; two sides to play; three difficulties. It uses GameMaker's `mp_grid` path finding, runtime tilemaps (a dual-grid tileset so rock edges are rounded), a shader that paints each side's colours, particle systems for explosions and smoke, a soft shroud drawn from a one-pixel-per-cell surface, and sound effects made in code. All names, art and characters are original. |
| [`snake_hangman3/`](snake_hangman3/) | v3: the site's homepage look (warp-speed stars, frosted glass, crew pods), plus four stages with walls, space debris on paths, Han's hint orb, Artsy reviving letter by letter, a particle starfield, a shader on the play field and sound effects made in code. |
